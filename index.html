Dear Malkin,

I still remember the first time I saw you.

You seemed like an angel who had somehow landed on Earth from heaven. The moment I saw your beautiful, celestial face, I fell for you harder than I ever thought I could. And honestly, even now, sometimes I look at you and wonder how someone like you could choose someone like me.

But then I think maybe, somehow, God blessed me that day.

I love everything about you. Your smile. Your voice. The way you get happy over little things. Your mood swings. I love you when you're happy, I love you when you're sad, I love you when you're angry, and yes, I even love you when you're cursing me. 😭❤️

I just love you.

Every single time I see you, I can't help but smile. Sometimes I look at you and genuinely think, "How can someone be this beautiful?" You're my goddess, my Malkin, and someone I feel incredibly lucky to have in my life.

There are moments when I feel like I don't deserve you. But then another thought comes into my mind—that maybe nobody could love you the way I do. Because what I feel for you is something I can't even properly explain.

These seven months with you have honestly felt like heaven.

And if I could ask for one thing, it would be that we get to keep feeling this way for a very, very long time. I want to spend my future with you. I want a happy, peaceful life with you. I want more ordinary days, more stupid conversations, more laughs, more arguments, more memories, and more of us.

There's one thing I sometimes feel bad about.

Sometimes I can't get you the things you love or the things you deserve, and that hurts me because I wish I could give you everything. I'm not there yet. I'm still figuring things out, and sometimes money is tight. But please never mistake what I can't buy for what I can't give.

Because my love for you isn't measured by what I can afford.

If I were there with you today, I wouldn't need this letter to explain everything. I'd rather look at you and show you, in every possible way, just how much you mean to me.

But since I can't be there right now, I made this little corner of the world for you.

And I hope when you read this, you smile.

Happy Birthday, Shruti.

Thank you for these seven beautiful months.

Thank you for choosing me.

And thank you for being you.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like, but I know what I want it to look like.

You and me. Together.

I LOVE YOU MORE THAN ANYTHING, MY GIRL. ❤️

Forever the one who's hopelessly in love with you. ❤️
